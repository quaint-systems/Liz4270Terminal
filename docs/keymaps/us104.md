# US 104-key (ANSI) (`us104`)

Status: bench-tested: Inland 104-key 1a86:0600 on the Fruit Jam rig, Oct 2026.

Chosen for any keyboard that no other keymap claims.

Generated by `tools/sim/keymap_doc.py` from `firmware/core/keymaps/us104.c`; rerun it rather than editing this page.

Each row is a key or chord on the USB keyboard. **3483-V key** and **Code** are what the terminal sends, as captured from a real 3483-V. **3270 modifiers** are the Shift and Alt it sends the key with: *kept* means the PC's own. **oec reads** is what oec's typewriter keymap makes of the whole press, as a cross-check. A Ctrl chord is Ctrl arriving in the same USB report as its key.
